About Kudu

Kudu is a fork of the established Liquity v1 protocol, designed to provide decentralized and efficient liquidity solutions for users. Our community-driven platform ensures maximum transparency and optimal yield through innovative protocols.

With Kudu, you can enjoy:

  • Zero price impact swaps
  • Advanced liquidity mechanisms
  • Robust decentralized governance
  • Community-focused rewards

Core Features

  • Lower fees compared to traditional platforms
  • Innovative yield farming opportunities
  • Stability through backed assets
